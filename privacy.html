Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Summary

VEEX is a community for collectors. We only collect what VEEX needs to work. We do not sell your personal data, we do not share it for advertising and we do not show ads. Payments are handled by Stripe: VEEX never sees your full card number. This policy applies to the VEEX app, veexapp.com and REEX. Last updated: October 23, 2026.

2. Information you give us

Account: your email and password (the password is stored only as a one-way hash; nobody, not even VEEX, can see it). Profile: username, name, avatar or photo, bio, city, country, categories, languages, shipping methods, preferred currency and any social handles you choose to add. Content and activity: listings, offers, wanted posts, Gallery posts, likes and comments, Studio projects and their links, forum posts, groups, messages and photos, trades, orders, reviews, wishlist, Vault items, friends, blocks and reports. Away mode: the return date and the short message you write. Account verification: the proofs you email us when you ask for the Verified badge. High-volume sellers: if you make 200 or more sales and $5,000 or more in sales in 12 months, a U.S. law (the INFORM Consumers Act) requires us to collect and verify your bank account details, a tax ID, and a working email and phone number; part of this is collected by Stripe when you set up payouts. If your sales reach $20,000 or more in a year, the law also requires us to show buyers your name, physical address and contact details. We use this only to comply with that law and keep it as long as it requires.

3. Payments, orders and shipping

When you buy, you pay on a secure Stripe page. Stripe receives your card details and VEEX never sees them; we only receive the result of the payment and the shipping address you enter there. We share that address with the seller so they can ship your item. To sell, you set up payouts with Stripe; Stripe collects the identity and bank details it requires by law, and VEEX only receives the status of your payout account. For each order we keep the item, price, fees, shipping address, carrier, tracking number and status. VEEX+ memberships are also billed by Stripe; the free trial does not ask for a card.

4. Information collected automatically

Like any website, our providers receive technical data when you use VEEX: IP address, device and browser type, and dates and times of access. We use it to deliver the service, keep it secure, prevent fraud and fix errors. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We only save on your own device your preferences (theme, language, text size, data saver), the login session that keeps you signed in, small notes so the app does not repeat a notice you already saw, and, until you close the app, temporary links to the private photos you opened. The camera of the barcode scanner works on your device: the images are not sent to VEEX.

5. What other people can see

Your public profile (username, avatar, bio, level, stats, badges, categories, your city if you allow it, and whether you are away), your listings, wanted posts, Gallery posts, comments, forum posts and the Studio projects you mark as public. Studio projects marked "Friends only" are visible only to your friends. Your Vault is visible to your friends, or to everyone if you choose. Your wishlist is private. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation, except that if someone reports a message, the VEEX team can see that message and its photo to review the report. An offer is visible only to the buyer and the seller. In an order, the seller sees the buyer's name and shipping address, and both sides see the order details.

6. How we use your information

To run VEEX: create and protect your account; show your profile, posts and collection; deliver messages and notifications; process payments, orders, refunds and disputes; match your wishlist with new listings; calculate reputation and levels; send service emails (like confirming your account, resetting your password or updates about an order); keep the community safe (blocks, reports, moderation, fraud prevention and account verification); review entries to campaigns like Founding Sellers; and improve the app. Reports are anonymous to the reported person. Verification proofs are used only for that review and deleted afterwards. An AI assistant helps our team sort reports and bug reports: it only sees the report and the reported content (never your email or password), it suggests what to do, and every decision about an account is made by a person. Reports about minors or intimate images are never processed by the AI assistant: only a person on our team reviews them. We do not use your data to train AI models or to build advertising profiles.

7. Service providers

We work with companies that process data only to provide their service to VEEX: Supabase (database, login and photo storage); Cloudflare (hosting and delivery of the app, and the anti-bot check when you sign in or sign up); Stripe (payments, refunds, payouts to sellers and VEEX+ billing); EasyPost (package tracking: the carrier and tracking number of an order); Resend (service emails); ImprovMX (forwarding of emails sent to @veexapp.com); Anthropic (the AI assistant that helps review reports; it does not use that data to train its models). When you scan a barcode or search for an item by name, only that number or the words you type are sent to public catalogs (MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, Open Library, Google Books) to fill in the details; your device connects to them directly, so they also receive your IP address. When book data comes from Google Books, the app says so. Music and video players (Spotify, YouTube and others) load only when you tap Play and are covered by those companies' own policies; for YouTube links, a preview image is loaded from YouTube before that. To check whether a reported listing photo was copied from the internet, our team may search for that photo with Google Lens.

8. REEX, our reading app

REEX is a reading app from the makers of VEEX that uses the same account. If you use REEX, your books, reading sessions, notes and settings are saved in the same secure database, and only you can see them. REEX reads your VEEX username, avatar and VEEX+ status, and it only adds a book to your Vault when you ask it to. Deleting your VEEX account also deletes everything you saved in REEX. The automatic spelling checker in REEX works on your device: what you write is not sent anywhere. Two writing tools use outside services only when you tap them: "Check spelling and grammar" sends the chapter you are checking to LanguageTool (LanguageTooler GmbH, Germany), and the dictionary sends the word you look up to Free Dictionary API; each handles that text under its own privacy policy. Word files you import and books you export are read and created on your device.

9. When we share information

Besides the providers listed above and what other users can see, we only share information when the law requires it (for example, a valid court order or a report of child sexual abuse material to NCMEC), to protect the safety of people or prevent fraud, or if VEEX joins or is acquired by another company, in which case this policy will keep protecting your data and we will let you know first. We never sell your data.

10. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed in the United States. It always travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). If you use VEEX from another country, your data is transferred to the United States to provide the service.

11. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, listings, posts, comments, Vault, private messages, trades, offers, reviews and settings, and the app deletes your photos (if a photo cannot be deleted at that moment, we remove it later). Some things remain: messages you sent in groups stay in the group, signed as "deleted_user" (your username is removed); records of orders and payments (item, amount, date, usernames and payment references) are kept as long as tax, accounting and anti-fraud laws require; content that was reported is kept as evidence while the report is reviewed, and reports about minors or intimate images are kept as the law requires (for example, at least one year for reports to NCMEC); and Stripe keeps its own records of your payments, your VEEX+ billing and your payout account under its policy. You cannot delete your account while it is suspended, has an order in progress or has an open report. Shipping addresses on orders are deleted 90 days after the order is closed; we keep only the state and country, which sales tax records require. Copies in our providers' short-term backups, if any, are removed within 30 days.

12. Your rights and choices

In Settings you can edit your profile, choose who sees your Vault and who can message you, hide your city, turn notifications off, block users, turn on away mode and delete your account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to privacy@veexapp.com; we answer within 30 days and we may ask you to confirm it is you. If you live in California, the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with privacy laws, you have these rights and we honor them, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell or "share" personal data as those terms are defined by California law.

13. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules on every table of our database, two-step verification (required for the VEEX team and to manage payouts, optional for everyone else), an anti-bot check when you sign in or sign up, and passwords stored only as a one-way hash. No online service is 100% secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you without undue delay.

14. Age requirement

VEEX is only for people 18 or older (or the age of majority where they live). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor created an account, tell us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, we will let you know in the app or by email before the change applies. The date at the top always shows the latest version.

16. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@veexapp.com.
