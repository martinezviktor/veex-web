Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Summary

VEEX is a community for collectors. We only collect what VEEX needs to work. We do not sell your personal data, we do not share it for advertising and we do not show ads. Payments are handled by Stripe: VEEX never sees your full card number. This policy applies to the VEEX app and veexapp.com. Last updated: November 23, 2026.

2. Information you give us

Account: your email and password (the password is stored only as a one-way hash; nobody, not even VEEX, can see it). Profile: username, name, avatar or photo, bio, city, country, categories, languages, shipping methods, preferred currency and any social handles you choose to add. Content and activity: listings, offers, wanted posts, Gallery posts, likes and comments, Studio projects and their links, forum posts, groups, messages and photos, trades, orders, reviews, wishlist, Vault items, friends, blocks and reports. Pro and seller features: the people you follow, the drops you ask to be reminded of ("Notify me"), the waitlists you join, the commission requests you send or receive (the description, budget and reference photos) and their quotes, the photos you add to a dispute, the carrier and tracking number you add in a protected trade, and the confirmation that an order of $500 or more was shipped with signature and insurance. Away mode: the return date and the short message you write. Account verification: the proofs you email us when you ask for the Verified badge. High-volume sellers: if you make 200 or more sales and $5,000 or more in sales in 12 months, a U.S. law (the INFORM Consumers Act) requires us to collect and verify your bank account details, a tax ID, and a working email and phone number; part of this is collected by Stripe when you set up payouts. If your sales reach $20,000 or more in a year, the law also requires us to show buyers your name, physical address and contact details. We use this only to comply with that law and keep it as long as it requires.

3. Payments, orders and shipping

When you buy, you pay with a card on a secure Stripe page. Stripe receives your card details and VEEX never sees them; we only receive the result of the payment and the shipping address you enter there. We share that address with the seller so they can ship your item. To sell, you set up payouts with Stripe; Stripe collects the identity and bank details it requires by law, and VEEX only receives the status of your payout account. For each order we keep the item, price, fees, shipping address, carrier, tracking number and status. If the seller scans or uploads the shipping label, we also keep a photo of it and, for a PDF label, the name and address printed on it (the buyer's), so VEEX can check a dispute or a chargeback; only the seller and the VEEX team can see them. They are deleted 150 days after the payment (the time a bank can take to send a chargeback, plus a margin); if a dispute, chargeback or VEEX review is still open then, they are kept until it is closed and then deleted. The postal code read from the label is deleted one year after the payment; the tracking number stays with the order record. For a protected local pickup, we create a 6-digit handover code that only the buyer can see in the app; the seller only types it to confirm the handover. VEEX+ and VEEX Pro memberships are also billed by Stripe; the free trial does not ask for a card.

4. Information collected automatically

Like any website, our providers receive technical data when you use VEEX: IP address, device and browser type, and dates and times of access. We use it to deliver the service, keep it secure, prevent fraud and fix errors. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We only save on your own device your preferences (theme, language, text size, data saver), the login session that keeps you signed in, small notes so the app does not repeat a notice you already saw, and, until you close the app, temporary links to the private photos you opened. The barcode scanner you use to look up an item works on your device: those images are not sent to VEEX. The shipping-label scanner is different: when a seller scans or uploads a shipping label, it is read on the device and a photo of it is saved privately, as explained in section 3. When a listing or a Gallery post stays on your screen for about a second (for example, while you scroll a list) or you open it, we record that your account saw it (once per item; your own items are not counted), to show its owner how many people saw it; the owner only sees the total, never who. These records are deleted when you delete your account. If you allow notifications in the VEEX phone app, we save a push token for your device, its platform (iPhone or Android) and your language, to send you notifications. It is deleted when you log out on that device or delete your account. You can turn off chat and group chat alerts in Settings → Notifications, or all of them in your phone's settings. To understand how VEEX is used, we record the searches made in the app (the words, the section and the number of results, without who searched), and once a day which platform, language and theme each account used. These records are deleted after 13 months. When an account is deleted, we keep only how old it was, its plan and its country, with nothing that identifies the person, for 2 years.

5. What other people can see

Your public profile (username, avatar, cover, bio, level, stats, badges, categories, your city and country only if "Show my city" is on (it starts off for new accounts, and when it is off nobody sees your city or your country; you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy), and whether you are away), your listings, wanted posts, Gallery posts, comments, forum posts and the Studio projects you mark as public. Your public profile also includes, if you added them, your full name, your social handles (Instagram, Discord), your languages, since when you collect and your shipping methods, plus your reputation, rating and number of trades, your VEEX+ or PRO badge and since when you have it, whether you can receive protected payments and how many followers you have. Anyone can see your public profile, your active listings, your public Studio projects and your commission menu, even without a VEEX account (for example on your shop page, app.veexapp.com/?shop=username). When you follow someone, they get a notification with your username and can see that you follow them; the number of followers is public, but not who they are. Drop reminders and waitlists are private: the seller does not see who joined them. Studio projects marked "Friends only" are visible only to your friends. Your Vault is visible to your friends, or to everyone if you choose. Your wishlist is private. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation (push notifications pass through the providers in section 7), except that if someone reports a message, the VEEX team can see that message and its photo to review the report. An offer is visible only to the buyer and the seller. In an order, the seller sees the buyer's name and shipping address, and both sides see the order details and the photos added to a dispute; the VEEX team sees them to review it. In a commission, the creator sees your request and your reference photos, and the VEEX team may look at them to review a report or a dispute. In a protected trade, each person sees the carrier and tracking number of the other.

6. How we use your information

To run VEEX: create and protect your account; show your profile, posts and collection; deliver messages and notifications; process payments, orders, refunds and disputes; match your wishlist with new listings; calculate reputation and levels; send service emails (like confirming your account, resetting your password or updates about an order); keep the community safe (blocks, reports, moderation, fraud prevention and account verification); review entries to campaigns like Founding Sellers; and improve the app. Reports are anonymous to the reported person. Verification proofs are used only for that review and deleted afterwards. Our team uses AI tools from Anthropic to help run VEEX: to check that payments, orders and the app work, find and fix bugs, prepare changes to the app, prepare accounting and safety reports, help sort reports and read the emails sent to our support addresses. For this work they use a read-only access that cannot change anything. With it they can read account and activity data such as usernames, listings, orders (without names or shipping addresses), trades, reviews, public posts, Vault items and reports, and the emails you send us. They cannot read your password, the email address of your account (they only see it in an email you send to our support addresses), your shipping address, your payment or bank details, your private and group messages, the messages in offers, trades and commission requests, commission reference photos, the texts and photos of disputes, your wishlist, your drop reminders and waitlists, your notifications, the people you block, a city or country you chose to hide, or the content of reports about minors or intimate images. Any change to the app or to our database that an AI tool prepares is reviewed and approved by a person on our team before it is applied. Reports about minors or intimate images are reviewed only by a person on our team. The AI tools suggest what to do; every decision about an account, a report or an order is made by a person. We have turned off the setting that would let Anthropic use this data to train its models. We do not use your data to train AI models or to build advertising profiles.

7. Service providers

We work with companies that process data only to provide their service to VEEX: Supabase (database, login and photo storage); Cloudflare (hosting and delivery of the app, and the anti-bot check when you sign in or sign up); Stripe (payments, sales tax calculation, refunds, payouts to sellers and VEEX+ and VEEX Pro billing); EasyPost (package tracking: the carrier and tracking number of an order); Resend (service emails); Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service (to deliver push notifications to the VEEX phone app: they receive your device's push token and the text of each notification, which for a chat or group message includes the sender's username and the beginning of the message); ImprovMX (forwarding of emails sent to @veexapp.com); Anthropic (the AI tools our team uses to help run VEEX, as described in section 6). When you scan a barcode or search for an item by name, only that number or the words you type are sent to public catalogs (MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, Open Library, Google Books, Pokémon TCG API, TCGdex, Scryfall, YGOPRODeck, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons) to fill in the details, and the result images are loaded from them (Cover Art Archive images come from the Internet Archive, archive.org); your device connects to them directly, so they also receive your IP address. If you choose a catalog image for an item in your Vault, VEEX saves a copy of it with that item and marks it as a catalog image; catalog images are only for your Vault, because a listing needs your own photos (see section 5 of the Terms). When book data comes from Google Books, the app says so. Music and video players (Spotify, YouTube and others) load only when you tap Play and are covered by those companies' own policies; for YouTube links, a preview image is loaded from YouTube before that. To check whether a reported listing photo was copied from the internet, our team may search for that photo with Google Lens.

8. When we share information

Besides the providers listed above and what other users can see, we only share information when the law requires it (for example, a valid court order or a report of child sexual abuse material to NCMEC), to protect the safety of people or prevent fraud, or if VEEX joins or is acquired by another company, in which case this policy will keep protecting your data and we will let you know first. We never sell your data.

9. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed in the United States. It always travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). If you use VEEX from another country, your data is transferred to the United States to provide the service.

10. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, listings, posts, comments, Vault, private messages, trades, offers, reviews and settings, and the app deletes your photos (if a photo cannot be deleted at that moment, we remove it later). We also delete your sign-in (email and encrypted password). Some things remain: messages you sent in groups stay in the group, signed as "deleted_user" (your username is removed); records of orders and payments (item, amount, date, usernames and payment references) are kept as long as tax, accounting and anti-fraud laws require; content that was reported is kept as evidence while the report is reviewed, and reports about minors or intimate images are kept as the law requires (for example, at least one year for reports to NCMEC); photos you added to a dispute on an order, and reference photos you sent with a commission request, are kept as evidence of that order or request and cannot be deleted by you; and Stripe keeps its own records of your payments, your VEEX+ billing and your payout account under its policy. You cannot delete your account right away while it is suspended, has an order in progress, has money from a sale still on its way to your bank, has a protected trade in progress or in dispute (or one you cancelled in the last 7 days), has a VEEX+ or Pro plan that is still being charged (cancel it first in VEEX+ → Manage subscription on app.veexapp.com) or has an open report (or a report about minors or intimate images closed less than one year ago); to confirm the deletion you type your password again and, if you turned on two-step verification, your 2-step code. If something is still pending (except a suspension), you can schedule the deletion instead: we delete the account automatically as soon as nothing is pending and email you when it is done, and if you have a VEEX+ or Pro plan we cancel it at the end of the time you already paid, with no more charges. Until then you can keep using VEEX, or cancel the scheduled deletion in Settings → Delete my account. If you cancel it after we cancelled your plan, the plan still ends on that date; you can choose a plan again in VEEX+. Shipping addresses on orders are deleted 90 days after the order is closed; we keep only the state and country, which sales tax records require. The photo of a scanned shipping label, and the name and address read from it, follow their own schedule (section 3), even if the account is deleted before then. If an account received copyright strikes, we keep a one-way fingerprint of its email (not the email itself) with those strikes, so a repeat infringer cannot simply start over. If the account used the Pro Day One price, we keep the same kind of fingerprint with that spot, so the offer counts once per person and email even after the account is deleted. If the account owed VEEX $5 or more in fees when it was deleted, we keep the same kind of fingerprint with that amount and its order, only to collect it if the same person comes back. Photos you upload to a listing, a post or your profile but never use there are deleted automatically after about 24 hours. Copies in our providers' short-term backups, if any, are removed within 30 days.

11. Your rights and choices

In Settings you can edit your profile, choose who sees your Vault and who can message you, hide your city, turn notifications off, block users, turn on away mode and delete your account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to privacy@veexapp.com; we answer within 30 days and we may ask you to confirm it is you. If you live in California, the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with privacy laws, you have these rights and we honor them, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell or "share" personal data as those terms are defined by California law.

12. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules on every table of our database, two-step verification (required for the VEEX team and to manage payouts, optional for everyone else), an anti-bot check when you sign in or sign up, and passwords stored only as a one-way hash. No online service is 100% secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you without undue delay.

13. Age requirement

VEEX is only for people 18 or older (or the age of majority where they live). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor created an account, tell us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, we will let you know in the app or by email before the change applies. The date at the top always shows the latest version.

15. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@veexapp.com.
