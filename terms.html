(c) The seller must ship within 3 days of payment and add a tracking number; if the item is not shipped within 72 hours, the order is cancelled automatically and the buyer gets a full refund. Orders of $250 or more must be shipped with signature on delivery and insurance for the full value; the seller confirms it when adding the tracking number. (d) When the seller scanned or uploaded the shipping label in the app and the carrier marks the package delivered, the buyer has 48 hours to report a problem (5 days for orders of $500 or more); if the buyer does nothing, or confirms receipt, the payment is released to the seller. We remind the buyer 24 hours before. If the seller has fewer than 5 completed sales, the payment is released when the buyer confirms receipt, or 7 days after delivery if the buyer does nothing. If the carrier shows delivery to a different ZIP code than the buyer's address, the payment stays on hold until the issue is resolved. The payment is also not released automatically when the delivery cannot be confirmed (for example, the carrier gives no ZIP code, or the package was delivered or moving before the payment), or for orders of $500 or more: in those cases it is released when the buyer confirms receipt, or after VEEX reviews the order. The same applies when the seller typed the tracking number instead of scanning or uploading the label, or when an order has no automatic tracking (for example, the seller chose "Other" as the carrier, or the carrier's tracking cannot be followed automatically). If the buyer has not confirmed receipt or reported a problem 10 days after the item was shipped, we remind the buyer to do it; if the buyer still does not answer, VEEX reviews the order and may release the payment to the seller based on the tracking and the other evidence. (e) Seller fees, deducted from the payout (sales tax is never part of the payout to the seller or of these fees): the VEEX fee of 8% of the item price plus the shipping cost (minimum $1.50; 6% for VEEX Pro members whose membership is paid and up to date, or during free Pro time from the Founding Sellers campaign or a Pro gift code, set when the buyer opens the payment page), plus payment processing of 2.9% + $0.30 on the item price plus the shipping cost. The shipping cost is paid out to the seller to cover the label. The buyer does not pay these fees. After the payment is released, Stripe sends the money to the seller's bank account; it usually arrives within a few business days. Protected payments are charged in U.S. dollars; if your card is in another currency, your bank may charge a conversion or foreign transaction fee. We may change the fees with at least 30 days' notice in the app or by email; new fees never apply to orders already paid. (f) Protected local pickup: if the listing allows pickup, the buyer can pay on VEEX and meet the seller in person. The buyer gets a 6-digit code and gives it to the seller only after receiving the item and checking it; the seller enters it in Orders and the payment is released. If they do not complete the handover within 7 days, the VEEX team reviews the order with both of them and releases or refunds the payment. Never give your code before you have the item. (g) Drops and preorders (VEEX Pro sellers): a drop is a listing announced with a date and time; nobody can buy it before then, and its followers are notified when it is announced and when it goes live (at most one alert of each kind per seller every 6 hours). A preorder is paid now and the seller promises a ship-by date (up to 90 days); the payment stays protected, and if the seller does not ship by that date the order is cancelled and the buyer is refunded in full. Before that date the seller can cancel and refund the order, or propose a later date: the buyer can accept it or cancel for a full refund, and if the buyer does not accept it the original date stays. Dates end at 11:59 PM New York time. Preorders cannot go in a bundle or be picked up in person. Commissions (VEEX Pro creators) are protected the same way, with their own limits: the creator sends a quote with a price and a ship-by date (up to 120 days after the quote), the buyer pays it protected, and if it is not shipped by that date the order is cancelled and refunded. The date is when the creator must ship the item, not when it arrives. The days the creator promised count from the payment, so if the buyer pays later the date moves too. In a dispute about a commission, the request and the quote are the description of the item. A quote expires if it is not paid within 14 days after it is sent, and a request expires if the creator does not send a quote within 30 days; nothing is charged for an expired quote or request. Preorders stop taking new orders 2 days before their ship-by date. (h) Listings with several units: a seller can list several identical units of an item. They are sold at the listed price only: offers are not available on them (see section 9). The seller can set a maximum number of units per buyer; using other accounts to get around it is not allowed (see section 13). When all units are sold or being paid for, you can join the waitlist: it only notifies you when a unit becomes available again, it does not save you a place or a unit, and the first person to pay gets it. On any listing, when a buyer opens the payment page the item (or one unit) is set aside for that buyer for up to 30 minutes; if the payment is not completed, it is available again. (i) VEEX does not provide escrow services; protected payments are a payment-processing service with buyer protection under these rules.
